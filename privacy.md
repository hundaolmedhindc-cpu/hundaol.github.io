# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

**App name:** Outreach Sequencer
**Developer:** Hundaol W. Medhin ("I", "me")
**Contact:** hundaolmedhin.dc@gmail.com

## What this app does
Outreach Sequencer is a personal-use tool that sends scheduled email
sequences from my own Gmail account and checks that account for replies.
It is not distributed to other users.

## Data the app accesses
When I connect my Gmail account, the app may access:
- My outgoing email, to send messages I have composed
- My inbox, to detect replies from recipients
No other person's account is ever connected.

## Data storage
Emails and status data (recipient address, send status, dates) are stored
only on my personal computer. Nothing is sold, shared, or transferred
to any third party.

## Data retention
Data is kept until I delete it locally. Revoking Gmail access
immediately stops all access.
